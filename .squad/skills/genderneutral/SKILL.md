# Gender-Neutral Writing

Use for German content that should avoid exclusionary wording.

## Key Rules

- Prefer inclusive and role-neutral terms where feasible.
- Avoid generic masculine defaults in mixed audiences.
- Keep readability high; do not force awkward constructions.
- Apply one consistent inclusive style per document.

## Review Checklist

- Biased or exclusionary terms replaced.
- Inclusive alternatives remain clear and natural.
- Terminology is consistent throughout the text.
