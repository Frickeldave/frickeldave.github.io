# Consistent Informal Address

Use for German user-facing copy in docs, blog, and content pages.

## Key Rules

- Use Du/Dein/Dir consistently.
- Avoid mixing with formal Sie/Ihnen/Ihr.
- Keep phrasing natural and readable.
- Preserve consistency within and across documents.

## Review Checklist

- No formal pronouns remain.
- Informal address is stable in headings, body, and examples.
- No mixed tone in the same document.
