# Compliance Checker

Use for legal/compliance audits and release-readiness checks.

## Audit Areas

- Impressum completeness.
- Datenschutz clarity and lawful basis references.
- Image attribution and copyright traceability.
- Accessibility statement quality and feedback path.
- Third-party tool and dependency license compatibility.

## Output Style

- Separate critical blockers, warnings, and recommendations.
- Distinguish legal must-have items from best-practice advice.
- Provide actionable remediation linked to concrete files/areas.

## Constraint

This is operational guidance, not legal advice.
