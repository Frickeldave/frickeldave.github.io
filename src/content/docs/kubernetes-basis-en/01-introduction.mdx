## What is this about?

This training teaches the fundamentals of Kubernetes in two hands-on days. The focus is not on
memorising terms, but on building a working container platform yourself -- step by step, inside your
own cluster.

Kubernetes is the de-facto standard platform for container orchestration in modern IT
infrastructure. Anyone who wants to run, scale and manage container applications reliably cannot get
around Kubernetes.

## What do you build by the end?

At the end of the two days, two real applications run inside your own Kubernetes cluster -- both
reachable behind a shared Ingress controller:

| Application      | Image                             | URL                                |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Chromium Browser | `lscr.io/linuxserver/chromium`    | chrome.k8s-training.frickeldave.de |
| Code-Server IDE  | `lscr.io/linuxserver/code-server` | code.k8s-training.frickeldave.de   |

Both applications come from [linuxserver.io](https://www.linuxserver.io/) -- a community that
maintains standardised, well-documented Docker images. You get visual web UIs right away and see
your progress in the cluster immediately.

On the second day a third application joins in optionally -- a real database with web
administration:

| Application         | Image                            | URL                                      |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| MariaDB (database)  | `lscr.io/linuxserver/mariadb`    | -- (only reachable internally via ClusterIP) |
| phpMyAdmin          | `lscr.io/linuxserver/phpmyadmin` | phpmyadmin.k8s-training.frickeldave.de   |

This exercise is designed as a **deep dive** and brings together every concept from Day 1 and Day 2
-- including PersistentVolumeClaim, Secret, init container and a custom Ingress route.

<Image src={knba01010} alt="2 containers behind an ingress controller" />

## Who is this training for?

The training targets IT professionals who want to use Kubernetes in their daily work: developers,
DevOps engineers and system administrators with Linux basics and a basic understanding of
containers (Docker basics).

Prior Kubernetes knowledge is not required -- the training starts at zero and builds all concepts
step by step.

## How is the training structured?

| Day   | Focus                        | Result at the end of the day                        |
| ----- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| [Day 1](/docs/kubernetes-basis-en/10-day-1) | Fundamentals & first steps   | Chromium and Code-Server run in the cluster, reachable via Ingress |
| [Day 2](/docs/kubernetes-basis-en/20-day-2) | Production readiness & networking | Chromium and Code-Server production-ready (resources, probes, rolling updates, PVC); optional: MariaDB + phpMyAdmin as a third application |
